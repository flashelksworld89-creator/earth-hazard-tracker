Geographical Ascendant Clock

The hour hand is the actual local Ascendant. The minute hand measures progress through the current 30° rising sign and the sweep ticker measures the current 3°20′ pada. A second ASC date/time system is anchored to each local 0° sidereal Aries rising. You can use the original ASC-day algorithm or switch on an adjustable epoch calendar that produces a completely separate month/day/year date.
